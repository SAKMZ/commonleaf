The argument is that behaviour follows identity rather than the other way
around. You do not read thirty books a year by setting a target of thirty
books; you read them by becoming the kind of person who reads.

## The four laws

| Law | Make it | To break a habit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | obvious | invisible |
| 2 | attractive | unattractive |
| 3 | easy | difficult |
| 4 | satisfying | unsatisfying |

## Quotes worth keeping

> You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your
> systems.

## Where it connects

The identity argument rhymes with [[Stoicism]] — both are more interested in
what you repeatedly do than in what you intend. The mechanics of making a habit
*easy* are the same mechanics as reducing friction in [[Deliberate Practice]].
